Caring for stoneware

Can it go in the oven?

Yes, up to 200°C — just avoid sudden temperature changes, like a cold dish into a hot oven.

Why does my piece look different from the photo?

Glazes move in the kiln. Your piece will share the colour and feel, never the exact pattern.
